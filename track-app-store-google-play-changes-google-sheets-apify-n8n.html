Track App Store and Google Play Changes in Google Sheets with Apify and n8n

Store pages change quietly: a new version, a price tweak, a rating that slides. Here is how to keep the apps you care about in one Sheet and get a row whenever something moves.

If you follow your own apps and a few competitors, you probably open each store page now and then and hope to notice what changed. Version numbers, prices and ratings differ by store and by country, so the checking multiplies fast. A scheduled workflow does it for you and writes only what changed.

Scope: the starter tracks the iOS and Android apps you list, in up to five countries. Charts are Apple's official top 100 Top Free and Top Paid only. Google Play charts and reviews, keyword rankings and ASO analysis are not included, and the starter does not store review rows.

The same app can look different in each store

The App Store & Google Play Tracker reads Apple's public lookup and chart feeds and the public Google Play app page. A real run on 12 October 2026, for the United States:

Both stores show the number each store reports. They are not directly comparable: audiences, prompts and rating rules differ. The point for tracking is that each app, store and country gets its own row, with its own history.

The same run also read Apple's US Top Free chart. The first three positions that day were Muse from Meta, Vinted: Pre-loved marketplace and ChatGPT. Chart positions move daily, so a threshold (five places by default) decides when a move counts as a change.

What counts as a change

The Actor reports a new version, a price or currency change, a rating that moved by at least your threshold (0.05 stars by default), a chart move or a chart entry or exit, and an app that the store no longer lists in a country. The first run marks every app NEW: your baseline. After that, apps that did not change are not returned at all, so a quiet day writes no rows. On 12 October 2026 a fresh app pair returned 2 NEW rows on the first call and 0 on the second.

The n8n workflow

A schedule starts one Actor run. Before anything is written, n8n checks the run status and the HEALTH report: failed lookups, blocked responses, the row count and the charge limit. If anything looks incomplete, the workflow stops and leaves your Sheet untouched. Otherwise it upserts the latest state of each app into an Apps tab and appends one row per change to a Changes tab, with the old and new values kept as JSON.

  1. Create Apps and Changes tabs and paste the header rows from the starter.
  2. Import the inactive workflow and add your own Apify and Google credentials.
  3. Enter your spreadsheet ID, your App Store ids or links, and your Google Play package names.
  4. Run manually twice. The first run saves every app as NEW; the second should save nothing.
  5. Enable the daily schedule only after both runs look right.

Limits worth knowing

Some Google Play apps ship different builds per device and show no version, so they are tracked by update date. A REMOVED row means the store no longer lists the app in that country, which is not proof the app was withdrawn. The Actor charges a small start fee plus a per-row fee, unchanged apps cost no row fee, and the starter caps each run at $0.10; check the Store page for current prices. The tracker's memory is shared by all tasks in your Apify account. On 12 October 2026 the whole workflow ran against a real Google Sheet (2 apps saved as NEW, 0 rows on the second run), and the Changes tab was checked with synthetic events. No real version, price or rating change has been observed during testing, and a scheduled (timer-triggered) run of this exact workflow has not been tested; the structure follows the career page starter, whose schedule was verified.
